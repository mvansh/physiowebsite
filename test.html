Does TPS replace my receptionist?
+
No. TPS works alongside your team to handle repetitive tasks like answering after-hours inquiries, sending automated reminders, and reactivating past patients, so your staff can focus on providing great in-clinic care.
How long does setup take?
+
Setup takes only 3 days. We build and configure everything for your clinic so you can go live without technical hassle or downtime.
Will patients have to download anything?
+
No. Patients don't need to download any apps. Everything runs directly through WhatsApp, making it effortless for them to communicate and book.
Can it work with my current WhatsApp?
+
Yes, it connects smoothly with your current number. If you don't have a dedicated business number yet, we recommend setting one up so your clinic messages stay separate and professional.
What if I already have a website?
+
TPS connects directly to your current site. We add a smart WhatsApp chat button that lets website visitors instantly start a conversation with your clinic on WhatsApp, converting your existing traffic into booked appointments immediately.
How much staff training is required?
+
Minimal to none. Because the automation works silently in the background, your team only needs a simple 15-minute walkthrough to understand how to view incoming bookings.
